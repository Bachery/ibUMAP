Algorithm
—

Change vs previous run. Clusters of two consecutive runs are matched one-to-one by maximum overlap (Hungarian assignment; noise stays noise). The share counts cells whose matched cluster differs, including changes to or from noise, as in the case-study repeatability evaluation. It includes splits and merges and is not a misclassification rate. When the cell count changes, the comparison uses the cells both runs contain.

Scope. This demo runs dimensionality reduction and HDBSCAN on a fixed subset of the BRAQUE proxy feature matrix. It is not part of the paper's results and does not reproduce expert phenotype annotation. An equal seed gives an equal result only when deterministic mode and all other settings are unchanged.
